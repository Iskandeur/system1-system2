How the two are asked

Jev gets a typed choice question: your question as the instruction, one criterion per answer (the answer itself, unless you write answer: criterion in the list). The LLM gets the same question and list in a prompt and must reply with JSON {"label": …}. Prices are OpenRouter's public list; the real cost shown after the run is what OpenRouter reports per call.

Recorded answers: Jev 1.13 via OpenRouter's decisions endpoint; GPT-5.2 via an Anthropic-compatible endpoint, priced at OpenRouter's public list price. Datasets: MASSIVE (CC BY 4.0), UCI Sentiment Labelled Sentences and SMS Spam Collection (CC BY 4.0), three synthetic sets generated by the repo. Everything reproducible from the repository; the rigorous version with confidence intervals is the study.
